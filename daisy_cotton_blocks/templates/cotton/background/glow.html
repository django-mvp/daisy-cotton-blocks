{% comment %} ============================================================ — two blurred discs of colour Goes in a block's `background` slot. Heavily blurred, low opacity, so the surface under the copy stays close to the page's own background and the text keeps the contrast it was designed for. That restraint is the whole point: colour on a saturated gradient is what fails on a theme you did not test. Attributes from the disc at the top, a semantic palette name. Default primary. to the disc at the foot. Default secondary. intensity faint | soft | bold — how far the discs come through. Default soft. `bold` is worth checking against a dark theme. class extra classes on the layer ============================================================ {% endcomment %}
