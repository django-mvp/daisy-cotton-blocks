That puts the background above the section's own surface colour and below every piece of copy, without anything in the slot needing to know its own z-index or position. Backgrounds compose with layouts freely: any background under any hero. The layer is hidden from assistive technology, so a background is decoration by definition. An image that carries meaning belongs in the `media` slot of `` or ``, where it is an with an `alt`. ── Why the actions are a slot ────────────────────────────────────────────── Not a pair of label/href attributes: a call to action is where a project needs full control — a form submit, an `hx-post`, a target, a tracking attribute — and building the link here would put an author-supplied URL inside an href this template is responsible for escaping, for no gain.
