With `` in the background slot it is also the image-backed hero. The attributes and slots are declared in the annotations above this block, which is what the component gallery reads to build its controls. Everything below is the reasoning those one-liners have no room for. ── What goes in the c-vars declaration, and what does not ────────────────── Props only. Slot names are deliberately absent, even though several component libraries list them there — Cotton fills a named slot whether or not it is declared, and the only thing declaring one buys is stopping an *attribute* of that name from reaching `{{ attrs }}`. Nobody passes `actions="..."` as an attribute. Leaving them out keeps one list meaning one thing: the declaration is the prop set, the `@prop` lines document it, and the two can be checked against each other mechanically. With slot names mixed in, every one of them reads as an undocumented prop. Note the spelling above: the tag's own name never appears in prose here, written out in angle brackets. The gallery's parser scans the raw source for the first occurrence and would read a mention inside a comment as the declaration itself, leaving every real prop reported as missing. ── The background slot ───────────────────────────────────────────────────── The section is a stacking context, and whatever goes in the slot is rendered into a layer at `-z-10` inside it. That puts the background above the section's own surface colour and below every piece of copy, without anything in the slot needing to know its own z-index or position. Backgrounds compose with layouts freely: any background under any hero. The layer is hidden from assistive technology, so a background is decoration by definition. An image that carries meaning belongs in the `media` slot of `` or ``, where it is an with an `alt`. ── Why the actions are a slot ────────────────────────────────────────────── Not a pair of label/href attributes: a call to action is where a project needs full control — a form submit, an `hx-post`, a target, a tracking attribute — and building the link here would put an author-supplied URL inside an href this template is responsible for escaping, for no gain. The layout is the hard part and the part this block owns. ============================================================ {% endcomment %}
