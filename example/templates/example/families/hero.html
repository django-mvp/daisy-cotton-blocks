Hero blocks

The block at the top of a page. Four of them, because the arrangement is fixed per block and a different arrangement is a different tag — tone and size change how a block looks, never where its pieces sit.

What every hero takes

The four blocks share one vocabulary, so moving a page from one to another is a tag change and nothing else.

Name Kind What it does
eyebrow attribute A short line above the heading — a category, a release name.
title attribute or slot The heading. Use the slot when it contains markup.
lead attribute or slot The sentence under the heading.
level attribute Heading level, 1 to 6. Defaults to 1, which is right for a landing page.
tone attribute base, soft, glow or neutral. The surface under the copy.
size attribute sm, md, lg — and screen on the two blocks it makes sense for.
announcement slot Sits above everything. A pill linking to the latest release, usually.
actions slot The button row. You write the links, so they can post, submit or carry anything.
footnote slot Under the buttons. "No card required", a rating, a row of logos.
media slot The visual, on the two blocks that have one. Anything at all.

Every colour on this page comes from the active theme. Switch it in the bar above and the blocks follow, including the glow and the marked words in the headings.
