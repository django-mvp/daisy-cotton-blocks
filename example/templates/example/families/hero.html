Hero blocks

The block at the top of a page. Three arrangements, because the arrangement is fixed per block and a different arrangement is a different tag — and a separate family of backgrounds that goes under any of them.

What every hero takes

The three blocks share one vocabulary, so moving a page from one to another is a tag change and nothing else.

Name Kind What it does
eyebrow attribute A short line above the heading — a category, a release name.
title attribute or slot The heading. Use the slot when it contains markup.
lead attribute or slot The sentence under the heading.
level attribute Heading level, 1 to 6. Defaults to 1, which is right for a landing page.
invert attribute Puts the copy in neutral-content, for a dark background.
size attribute sm, md, lg — and screen on c-hero.centred.
reverse attribute c-hero.split only. Swaps the two columns at wide widths. No effect once the block has stacked.
class attribute Lands on the outer section. Where a plain surface colour goes: bg-base-200.
background slot A background block, or any markup. Rendered into a layer behind the copy.
announcement slot Sits above everything. A pill linking to the latest release, usually.
actions slot The button row. You write the links, so they can post, submit or carry anything.
footnote slot Under the buttons. "No card required", a rating, a row of logos.
media slot The visual, on split and showcase. Anything at all.

How the background slot works

A hero is a stacking context, and whatever goes in the slot is rendered into a layer at -z-10 inside it: above the block's own surface colour, below every piece of copy. Nothing in the slot needs to know its own position or z-index, so any background composes with any layout.

The layer is hidden from assistive technology, which makes a background decoration by definition. An image that carries meaning goes in the media slot instead, where it is an <img> with an alt.

A dark background needs light copy, and a background cannot reach up to recolour the block above it — so the block carries invert and each background says whether it wants one.

The backgrounds themselves, their options and what they look like under the same hero are on the Backgrounds page.

Every colour on this page comes from the active theme. Switch it in the bar above and all of it follows. Where the markup says <c-demo.screenshot>, a real project puts its own screenshot.
