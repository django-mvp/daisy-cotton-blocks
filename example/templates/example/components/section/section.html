Why we stopped doing release days

For three years every release went out on a Thursday afternoon. Everybody kept the evening free, somebody ordered food, and the rollback plan was printed out. It worked, in the sense that nothing was ever lost.

It also meant a fix written on a Friday morning reached nobody for six days. This is how we got from one release a week to thirty a day, and what we would do differently.
