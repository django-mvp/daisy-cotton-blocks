A library of page blocks for Django projects running django-cotton and daisyUI. Heroes, section layouts, calls to action and the rest of the furniture a public-facing page needs.

Component libraries in this space cover the application: navigation, forms, tables, dialogs. They stop at the pages that sit in front of it, which get assembled by hand out of raw utility classes in every project, every time.

Blocks are configured through their attributes and take their colours from whatever daisyUI theme the project already runs. Change the theme from the bar above and everything on this page follows it, which is the behaviour every block is held to.

  • Not an application. No models, views, forms, URLs or migrations. A block's content comes from the template author, never from a queryset.
  • Not a CSS framework. daisyUI, its themes and the page reset all come from the project. This package ships only the plain utilities its own blocks need.
  • Not page templates. Blocks only. You pick the ones you want and assemble the page yourself.

No blocks are written yet. The pages in the sidebar hold a place for each family the roadmap plans, and each one renders the shell and nothing else until its blocks exist.
